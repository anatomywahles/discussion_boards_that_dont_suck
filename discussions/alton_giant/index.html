The Alton Giant

Course: Anatomy & Physiology II

Unit: Endocrine System

Discussion Type: Patient Story | Case Study | Ethics

Students explore the life of Robert Wadlow, the tallest person in recorded history, to understand how excess growth hormone affects the body and to consider the ethical implications of treating endocrine disorders.

Discussion Board Instructions

Part 1 – Learn About Gigantism & Robert Wadlow

Students begin by reading about gigantism and watching a video about Robert Wadlow, documenting the symptoms, causes, treatments, and impacts of his condition.

  • Identify organs and hormones involved in gigantism.
  • Describe common symptoms.
  • Investigate treatment options.
  • Analyze Robert Wadlow's experiences.

Part 2 – Original Discussion Post

Students identify three specific details from the Robert Wadlow video that stood out to them.

Then students answer ONE of the following questions:

  1. How did excess growth hormone affect Robert's physiology and daily life?
  2. How does Robert's story affect your understanding of the real-world impacts of endocrine disorders?
  3. If treatments had been available during Robert's childhood, should they have been used? Why or why not?

Part 3 – Peer Replies

Students reply to at least two classmates and extend the discussion through meaningful engagement and questions.
